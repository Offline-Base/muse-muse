{{TITLE}}, on a Muse Muse flash drive

Open "Start Here.html" in your browser first. It works offline.

Everything on this drive runs on your computer. Nothing is downloaded, and nothing
you type leaves your computer.

It runs on {{MIN_SPEC}}.

  Mac with Apple silicon:  double-click "Start (Mac).command"
  Windows PC (x64):        double-click "Start (Windows).cmd"

Start picks the build that fits your computer, offers to copy it there, and opens
the chat in your browser. Close its window to stop the model. Windows PCs with
Smart App Control on aren't supported yet.

To check the files, double-click "Verify (Mac).command" or "Verify (Windows).cmd".
They compare every file with SHA256SUMS and change nothing. A drive that arrived
broken, or a file that doesn't match? To get a new one, {{CONTACT_TEXT}}.

The drive doesn't run on Linux or on a Mac with an Intel chip. The model's page,
with its eval and Try it live, is {{PAGE_URL}}
