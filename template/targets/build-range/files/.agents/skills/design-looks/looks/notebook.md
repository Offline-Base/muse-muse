# Look: notebook

A warm paper notebook in afternoon light. Tactile off-white, soft hairline cards, one blue for the action, and pastel panels used like sticky notes. It suits planners, notes, habits, study and team tools.

## Palette
- Light: background `#f5f3ef`, surface `#ffffff`, text `#2b2926`, mutedText `#6b665f`, accent `#2463d1` with onAccent `#ffffff`, secondary `#a8442d`.
- Sticky-note panel fills (backgrounds only, text stays `#2b2926`): coral `#fbe3dc`, amber `#fbeccb`, sky `#dcebfa`, sage `#e3efdf`. Use at most two on a screen.
- Hairline `#e6e2db`.

## Type
- Display: `@fontsource-variable/fraunces` (family "Fraunces Variable") at 600, soft and warm, only for the h1 and section heads.
- Body: `@fontsource-variable/figtree` (family "Figtree Variable") at 400 and 600, generous line height (1.55).
- Verbs or key actions can sit in a small pill with a pale amber fill.

## Parts
- Cards: white, 1 px hairline, 12 px corners, no shadow, like ruled sections.
- Buttons: one blue filled button with 10 px corners; secondary buttons are text links or hairline outlines.
- The result surface: a sticky-note panel holding the real output (a plan, a list, a summary).
- Checklists: round checkboxes that fill blue with a small spring when ticked.

## Motion
Playful but brief: 200 ms ease, a slight spring on checks and on new cards appearing.

## Do
- Keep color as punctuation: one blue, a couple of pastel panels.
- Write copy that sounds like a person's notes.

## Don't
- No dark mode-only design, no heavy shadows, no more than two panel colors on one screen.
