# Look: voltage

Deep forest green with an electric lime. Massive heavy display type, pill shapes, and sections that flip between light and dark for rhythm. Confident and a little loud. It suits money transfers, travel, fitness, launches and anything that should feel energetic.

## Palette
- Light sections: background `#f3f5ef`, surface `#ffffff`, text `#14320b`, mutedText `#4a5843`, accent `#a6e36f` with onAccent `#14320b`, secondary `#14320b`.
- Dark sections: background `#14320b`, surface `#1d4212`, text `#f1f6ea`, mutedText `#bccbb2`, accent `#a6e36f` with onAccent `#14320b`.
- Soft gray-green surface for chips and inputs: `#e6ebe1`.

## Type
- Display: `@fontsource/archivo-black` (family "Archivo Black"), tightly tracked (-0.02em), used big: the h1 and key numbers only.
- Body: `@fontsource-variable/dm-sans` (family "DM Sans Variable") at 400 and 600.

## Parts
- Buttons, tabs, segmented controls and chips: full pills. The primary is lime with forest text.
- Cards and inputs: 12 px corners on the soft gray-green surface, no borders.
- The result: a dark forest panel with the key figure in lime display type.

## Motion
Snappy: 160 ms slides, a small scale-up on the primary button press.

## Do
- Alternate a light section with one dark section per screen at most.

## Don't
- No gradients, no thin light display weights, no extra accent colors.
