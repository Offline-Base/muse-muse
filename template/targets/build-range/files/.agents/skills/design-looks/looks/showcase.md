# Look: showcase

A white gallery where the product's output is the event. Huge result media in the center, compact quiet type around it, one blue for links and the main control, and big rounded frames with no shadows. It suits image and video makers, product photos, design tools and anything visual.

## Palette
- Light: background `#ffffff`, surface `#f4f4f6` (feature bands), text `#1b1b1d`, mutedText `#66666b`, accent `#0b63ce` with onAccent `#ffffff`, secondary `#1b1b1d`.
- Cards on the gray band are pure white.

## Type
- Display and body: `@fontsource-variable/onest` (family "Onest Variable"), headlines at 600 and compact, body at 400.
- Captions in mutedText at 13 px under every media frame.

## Parts
- Media frames: 24 to 28 px corners, edge to edge on phones, the result as large as the screen allows.
- Bands: alternate white sections with `#f4f4f6` bands holding oversized white cards.
- Buttons: compact blue pills for the main action, blue text links for the rest.

## Motion
Slow and smooth: 300 ms fades and gentle scale-ins as media loads.

## Do
- Give the output room. One message per section.

## Don't
- No dashboards of small cards, no shadows, no colored backgrounds besides the gray band.
