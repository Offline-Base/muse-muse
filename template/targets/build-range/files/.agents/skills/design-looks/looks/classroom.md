# Look: classroom

A playful storybook classroom. White paper, chubby rounded type, thick outlines, and buttons that feel like stickers pressed onto the page. One green means correct and progress. It suits learning, quizzes, kids and families, habits and games.

## Palette
- Light: background `#ffffff`, surface `#f5f7f3`, text `#2c3631`, mutedText `#5b6762`, accent `#2b7d1b` with onAccent `#ffffff`, secondary `#1769c4` (links and secondary actions).
- Feedback: correct is the accent green; wrong is `#c62828` with white text; a warm `#ffc83d` fill for streaks and stars, always with dark text.

## Type
- Display: `@fontsource-variable/fredoka` (family "Fredoka Variable") at 600, big and round.
- Body: `@fontsource-variable/nunito` (family "Nunito Variable") at 500 and 700, calm mid-gray for long text.

## Parts
- Buttons: pills or 16 px corners, a 2 px border, and a 4 px solid bottom edge one shade darker so they look pressable; pressing moves them down 2 px.
- Cards: 16 px corners with a 2 px light border.
- Progress: a thick rounded bar in green; answer options as big tappable tiles.
- Drawn shapes and simple illustrations in flat color, never photos of people.

## Motion
Bouncy and rewarding: a spring on correct answers, a small shake on wrong ones, 250 ms.

## Do
- Make every tap give feedback. Keep words short and friendly.

## Don't
- No thin type, no hairline-only controls, no dark mode-only design, no mascots that copy a real character.
