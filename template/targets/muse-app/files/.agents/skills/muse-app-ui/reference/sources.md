# Sources

Where the values in `tokens.md` come from, read on October 31, 2026. Values are facts from these sources, written in our own words. Nothing here is a logo, a mark, a name, a typeface or copy to reuse.

## The page this look follows (M)

- www.meta.ai, Meta's assistant on the web, read signed out in headless Chromium at 1440 x 900 and 390 x 844, in light and dark: the computed styles and CSS variables of the page, the composer, the sidebar, menus and the segmented control. Nothing was typed or sent.
- What that page can't show (a signed-in conversation, the person's bubble, the steps panel) comes from the sample below.
- Its typeface is Meta's own and isn't open, so this look doesn't use it or name it in a font stack.

## A 2x capture of the signed-in app (S)

- Colors and sizes sampled from the pixels of a 2x screenshot of a signed-in conversation. Where the page above could be read too, the samples matched it exactly (the secondary text, the faint text, the 768 px column), so they are trusted where it can't reach.
- The purple of Muse Muse's own working mark, which this look's AI gradient starts from.

## A description of screenshots (E)

- A written description of screenshots that weren't kept. Its measurements are estimates, and one shape comes from it, with the page's small product demos: the 18 px photo radius. Its background claims about people and fonts weren't checked and aren't used.

## Ours (O)

- Chosen for this template: every value that keeps a text pair at WCAG AA in both modes, keeps controls 44 px tall for touch, or keeps the template's parts working (the field border, the dark fill under white text, the scrim, the AI gradient's end).

## Fonts

- Inter, https://rsms.me/inter/, SIL Open Font License 1.1, self-hosted from https://www.npmjs.com/package/@fontsource-variable/inter (5.3.0), the optical size files (`opsz.css`). Laid out against 18 strings of the measured page, Inter with its optical size axis and -0.01em tracking was within about 1% of their width on average; the weight-only Inter files were 3% off, and 11% on a headline.

## Accessibility

- WCAG 2.2 contrast minimum (4.5:1 for text) and non-text contrast (3:1), https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/.
