Open source notices for this product's media features

This product reads text aloud, makes videos and writes music in your browser with the parts below. The speech model
and its runtime are downloaded from Muse Muse the first time and kept by your browser. Nothing you make is uploaded for
them.

1. SpeechT5 text to speech (microsoft/speecht5_tts), as the ONNX conversion Xenova/speecht5_tts at revision
   1723781b8ce2d02f0400c8337be04ae8ee3d6d56 (8-bit files encoder_model_quantized.onnx and
   decoder_model_merged_quantized.onnx). MIT License. Copyright (c) Microsoft Corporation.
   https://huggingface.co/microsoft/speecht5_tts

2. SpeechT5 HiFi-GAN vocoder (microsoft/speecht5_hifigan), as the ONNX conversion Xenova/speecht5_hifigan at revision
   cf980c3610d7b7f20919960031066ef7905737bd (model_quantized.onnx). MIT License. Copyright (c) Microsoft Corporation.
   https://huggingface.co/microsoft/speecht5_hifigan

3. The voice: speaker embedding cmu_us_slt_arctic-wav-arctic_a0508 from CMU ARCTIC X-Vectors
   (Matthijs/cmu-arctic-xvectors at revision 5c1297a9eb6c91714ea77c0d4ac5aca9b6a952e5), MIT License, made from the
   CMU ARCTIC speech databases (Carnegie Mellon University, http://www.festvox.org/cmu_arctic/), which are free for any
   use.
