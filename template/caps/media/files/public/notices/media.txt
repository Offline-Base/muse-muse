Open source notices for this product's media features

This product makes videos, writes music and counts things in photos in your browser. The video writer, the MP4 writer,
the MIDI writer, the pitch finder and the photo tiling were written for Muse Muse. They use no third-party code and run
no model in the page. Counting in photos asks Meta's SAM 3.1 through Muse Muse.
