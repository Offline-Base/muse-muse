# Zainab Haddad: Should our school have a garden? Explain your opinion.

A garden at our school would be a great idea, and here is why.

To begin, a garden would help us learn. In science we are learning about ecosystems, and a garden is a small ecosystem. We could find spiders, worms, and ladybugs and see how they all depend on each other. Priyanca told our table that compost has millions of tiny living things in it, and I want to see that with a magnifying glass.

Next, a garden would help us eat healthy food. My family makes tabbouleh with lots of parsley and mint. Parsley is easy to grow, so we could grow it at school and make tabbouleh for a class party. Santi said he would bring the lemons.

Also, a garden would make our school look beautiful. Right now there is a lot of gray concrete. Green plants and colorful flowers would make people smile when they walk in.

Some people might say kids will not take care of it. But if every class had a job, like watering on Mondays or weeding on Fridays, it would work.

In conclusion, I think our school should have a garden because we would learn, eat healthy, and enjoy a beautiful school.
