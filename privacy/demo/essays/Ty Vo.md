# Ty Vo: Should our school have a garden? Explain your opinion.

I think we should have a garden at our school.

My bà nội (that means grandma) grows a lot of herbs in buckets behind our building. She grows mint, basil, and a plant called rau răm that smells really strong. She uses them in phở. When I help her I learn the names in Vietnamese. A school garden could help kids learn about food from other countries too.

A garden is also good for the earth. Plants clean the air and give food to bees and butterflies.

My mom works nights at a nail salon so she sleeps in the day and we can not have a garden at our place. A school garden would let kids like me who do not have a yard grow something.

Gardens also teach you to be careful. You have to water every day and pull the weeds before they get big.

I think the best plants would be herbs, beans, and cucumbers because they grow fast.

That is why I think we should have a garden. It would help a lot of kids.
