# Camila Restrepo: Should our school have a garden? Explain your opinion.

Our school should have a garden becuase gardens are good for everybody.

My big sister Luciana has a garden at her middle school and she brings home herbs like basil and cilantro. My mom puts them in the beans and it tastes so good. If we had a garden we could grow herbs to and take them home to our familys.

Also a garden is good for learning. We could learn about worms and dirt and how roots drink water. I like science but I like it more when we do stuff with our hands instead of only reading the book.

A garden can also teach us responsability. If you forget to water your plant it will die, so you have to remember. That is like having a pet but easier.

I also think it would make kids happy. When I am in my abuela's garden I feel calm and I forget about being mad. Maybe kids would get in less fights if they could go in a garden.

Some peaple say we dont have money for a garden. We could have a bake sale or ask the store to give us seeds.

So I think we should have a garden. It would be fun and we would learn alot.
