# Wren Achterberg: Should our school have a garden? Explain your opinion.

I think our school should have a garden, but only if it is a garden that everyone can use.

At my old school in Ohio, we had a garden, but the beds were on the ground. Since I use a wheelchair, I could not reach the plants, so I mostly just watched. That was not fair. If our school builds a garden, it should have raised beds that are tall enough for me and wide paths so my chair fits. That way every kid can help.

A garden is also great for learning. You can study insects, weather, and soil. At my old school we kept a chart of how much rain fell each week, and then we compared it to how fast the plants grew. It was like being a real scientist.

Another reason is that gardens make people happy. Even when I could only watch, I liked being outside with the plants and the sunshine. Colors and fresh air make a big difference.

Some people might say raised beds cost more money. That is true, but they also have fewer weeds and are easier on everyone's backs, even grown-ups.

In conclusion, our school should build a garden that every student can use. When everyone is included, everyone can grow.
