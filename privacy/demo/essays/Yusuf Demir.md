# Yusuf Demir: Should our school have a garden? Explain your opinion.

I think our school should have a garden because it can give kids more food and teach us things.

I get free lunch at school, and sometimes I am still hungry after. If we had a garden, we could grow fruits and vegetables and have them as a snack in the afternoon. Apples take a long time, but strawberries and carrots are faster.

A garden would also teach us about where food comes from. My dad says that in Turkey his family grew everything they ate, like tomatoes, peppers, and eggplants. Here we just buy it. I want to know how to grow it myself.

Gardens are also good for animals like bees and butterflies. We learned that bees help plants make fruit. Without bees we would not have a lot of foods.

Some kids might not want to work in the garden. That is OK, they could just come look at it. But I think most kids would want to help once they see it.

That is why I think our school should have a garden. It would help kids be less hungry and learn more.
