# Leopold Ferreira: Should our school have a garden? Explain your opinion.

I think our school should have a garden. Here are my reasons.

Reason one is my brother Tomas. He is in 7th grade and his school has a garden and he says it is the best part of school. He grew a watermelon one time. It was small but it was still a watermelon. I want to grow one too.

Reason two is that it is good for science. You can learn about plants and dirt and bugs. Bugs are important even if they are gross.

Reason three is that we could cook what we grow. My family loves to cook. On Sundays we make feijoada and my avó puts collard greens in it. If we grew collard greens at school I could bring some home.

Also it would be good for the bees and the birds. And it would look better than the dirt by the parking lot.

I think the hard part would be the summer because nobody is at school. Maybe the teachers could take turns.

So that is why I think we should have a garden. It would be really fun and we would learn a lot of stuff.
