# Benjamin Adeyemi: Should our school have a garden? Explain your opinion.

I think our school should have a garden. It would be good for the kids and for the earth.

One reason is that kids could learn where food comes from. A lot of kids think food just comes from the store. My dad is a mechanic at the city bus garage and he grew up on a farm, and he says when you grow food yourself you never waste it. I think he is right because if I worked hard on a pepper I would eat it.

Another reason is it would help the earth. Plants make oxygen and bees need flowers. We learned that bees are in trouble because there are not enough flowers. A garden could help the bees in our neighborhood.

A third reason is it would be fun. Recess is fun but sometimes there is nothing to do. If we had a garden some kids could dig and plant instead of just standing around. I would rather pull weeds than stand by the wall.

Some people might think the garden will get messy. But we could have a garden club to keep it clean. I would join it.

That is why I think our school should have a garden. Please say yes.
