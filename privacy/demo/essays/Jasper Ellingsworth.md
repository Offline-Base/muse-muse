# Jasper Ellingsworth: Should our school have a garden? Explain your opinion.

I think our school should have a garden for a few reasons.

My first reason is it would be fun. Kids like being outside and a garden is a way to be outside and learn at the same time.

My second reason is food. We could grow strawberries, which are my favorite. Strawberries from a garden taste better than strawberries from the store because they are fresh. We could also grow lettuce and cucumbers.

My third reason is that Mr. Delgado said he would help. Mr. Delgado is the custodian at our school and he knows a lot about plants. He has a garden at his house with a fig tree. When I asked him he said he would show us how to use the hose and where the sunny spots are.

Some people might say kids will step on the plants. We could put a little fence and make paths out of wood chips.

I think a garden would be a good idea. It would be fun and we would get strawberries and Mr. Delgado could help us. Thank you.
