# Rose Hallaway: Should our school have a garden? Explain your opinion.

I think our school should have a garden, and I already know what I would plant first: roses! My name is Rose, so everyone says I have to like them, and I really do.

But a garden is not just about flowers. It is also about learning. In a garden you can see how bees carry pollen from one flower to another. You can see how a seed grows roots and then a stem and leaves. That is way more interesting than looking at a picture in a book.

A garden would also help us eat better. If we grew cherry tomatoes and snap peas, kids could eat them as a snack. My grandpa says vegetables from your own garden taste sweeter, and I think he is right.

Another reason is that a garden gives kids a quiet place. Sometimes the playground is loud and crowded. A garden with a bench would be a nice place for kids who want to read or just think.

Some people might say roses have thorns and kids could get hurt. That is true, but we could plant them in the back and wear gloves. The other plants could be in the front.

In conclusion, our school should have a garden because it helps us learn, eat healthy, and relax. And it would be even better with roses.
