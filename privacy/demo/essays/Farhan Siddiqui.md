# Farhan Siddiqui: Should our school have a garden? Explain your opinion.

I think our school should have a garden, but we need to do it in a safe way.

A garden would be good because kids could learn about plants and insects. We could see how a seed turns into a plant. We could also learn about the parts of a flower, like the petals and the stem, and that would help us on the science test.

A garden would also make the school look nice. Right now the front of the school just has grass and a sign. Flowers and vegetables would look a lot better.

But there is one problem. Gardens have bees, and I am allergic to bee stings. Last year I got stung at the park and my face got puffy and I had to go to the doctor. So if we have a garden, I think it should have flowers that do not bring a lot of bees near the door, and kids who are allergic should be allowed to wear gloves and long sleeves.

Even with that problem, I still want a garden. I would like to be in charge of the tomatoes because tomatoes do not have a lot of bees. I would also make signs for every row so people know what is growing.

In conclusion, a garden is a good idea if we plan it carefully. That way everyone can enjoy it, even kids like me.
