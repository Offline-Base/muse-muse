# Hana Whitlock: Should our school have a garden? Explain your opinion.

Our school should have a garden. A garden would help students learn, help the environment, and help our community.

First, a garden would help students learn. Teachers could use the garden for science lessons about soil, water, and sunlight. They could also use it for math, like measuring the beds to find the area and perimeter. Even writing would be better, because we could keep garden journals and describe what we see.

Second, a garden would help the environment. Plants clean the air and give homes to butterflies, ladybugs, and bees. A compost bin next to the garden could turn our lunch scraps into soil instead of sending them to the landfill. Our class throws away a whole bag of food scraps every day.

Third, a garden would help our community. My mom is a nurse, and she says that a lot of families do not get enough fresh vegetables. If our school grew food, we could share it with families who need it.

Some people might say that a garden costs too much. However, seeds are cheap, and local stores sometimes donate soil and tools to schools. We could also hold a plant sale to raise money.

In conclusion, a garden would be a great addition to our school. It would make us smarter, greener, and kinder.
