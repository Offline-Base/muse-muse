# IEP and 504 plan notes, Room 14 (invented)

Confidential. For the classroom teacher and the case manager, Ms. Pruitt. Every student and plan here is invented.

## Gideon Pakalnis (S401186), IEP

- Eligibility: specific learning disability in reading and written expression.
- Services: 30 minutes a day in the resource room with Ms. Pruitt, 1:00 to 1:30.
- Accommodations: speech-to-text for first drafts; spelling is not scored on first drafts; extended time (time and a half) on writing tasks; word bank on the desk.
- Annual review: February.

## Quentin Beaumont (S401270), IEP

- Eligibility: speech or language impairment.
- Services: speech therapy twice a week, Tuesday and Thursday at 10:15, pulled out of writing block.
- Accommodations: sentence starters, graphic organizer for paragraphs, oral rehearsal before writing.

## Dashiell Kowalczyk (S401152), 504 plan

- Basis: ADHD, diagnosed in second grade.
- Accommodations: preferential seating near the teacher, a movement break every 20 minutes, standing desk option, tasks broken into chunks with check-ins.

## Maya Lindqvist (S401259), 504 plan

- Basis: asthma.
- Accommodations: inhaler access at all times, rest breaks in PE and recess, extra time on long writing tasks after a flare-up, indoor option on high-pollen days.

## Wren Achterberg (S401337), 504 plan
