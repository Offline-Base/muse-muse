# Ikaika Kealoha: Should our school have a garden? Explain your opinion.

Yes, I think our school should have a garden.

Before we moved here, my family lived on the Big Island in Hawaii. My uncle Keoni has a farm there where he grows taro, sweet potatoes, and bananas. When I visited him in the summer, he let me plant a row of taro. He told me that in Hawaii people say the land takes care of you if you take care of it. I think a school garden could teach kids that too.

A garden would also help kids try new foods. Most kids here have never eaten taro or fresh papaya. We could not grow papaya here because it is too cold, but we could grow sweet potatoes and squash. Kids are more willing to try something they grew themselves.

Gardens are also good exercise. Digging, carrying water, and pulling weeds is hard work. My uncle is really strong from working on his farm all day.

The only bad thing is that it gets cold here in the winter. But we could grow plants in the spring and fall, and maybe build a small greenhouse.

I think a garden would make our school feel more like home for kids like me who miss their old place. That is why I think we should have one.
