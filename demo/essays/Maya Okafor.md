# Maya Okafor: Should our school have a garden? Explain your opinion.

In my opinion, our school should have a garden because it would help us learn science, eat healthier, and work as a team.

The first reason is science. In third grade, our class planted lima beans in cups, and I was amazed when the roots came out. But the cups were so small that the plants died after two weeks. In a real garden, plants would have room to grow, and we could watch the whole life cycle. We could even test which spot gets the most sunlight.

The second reason is healthy eating. According to our health lesson, kids should eat five servings of fruits and vegetables a day, but most of us do not. When I helped my aunt harvest spinach, I ate it raw right out of the garden, and I usually hate spinach! Growing food makes you want to taste it.

The third reason is teamwork. There are two Mayas in our class, me and Maya L., and we always get mixed up. But we both love plants, so we already decided we would be garden partners. A garden needs lots of people working together, like planting, watering, and weeding.

Some might say that a garden would take time away from learning. However, a garden is learning, because we would use reading, math, and science out there.

To sum up, a garden would help our whole school. I really hope we get one.
