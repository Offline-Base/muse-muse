# Dashiell Kowalczyk: Should our school have a garden? Explain your opinion.

Everybody calls me Dash so I will write it like that. I think yes we should have a garden.

Gardens are cool. You can grow pumpkins. Me and Giddy want to grow the biggest pumpkin in the whole state and we would win a ribbon. My uncle grew one that was like 200 pounds I think. You need a lot of water for that.

Also worms. I like worms. We could have a worm bin and feed them old food from lunch. That would be gross but cool.

Gardens are also good for you because vegetables. I dont like most vegetables but I would eat a pumpkin if it was in a pie.

I think the garden should be by the playground so we can go there at recess. And it should have a fence so the dogs dont get in. One time a dog dug up my moms flowers and she was so mad.

Also we could sell the pumpkins and get money for a field trip. I want to go to the science museum again.

So yes a garden. Pumpkins and worms and money. That is my opinion.
