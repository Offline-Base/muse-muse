# Valentina Ostrowski: Should our school have a garden? Explain your opinion.

Our school should definitely have a garden. A garden would help us learn outside, give us healthy snacks, and make recess better.

First of all, a garden would help us learn outside. When we learned about the water cycle, we only drew diagrams. In a garden, we could see water soak into the soil and watch plants use it. My friend Rosie and I tried growing basil on our windowsill at home, and we learned more from that than from the worksheet.

Second, a garden would give us healthy snacks. Instead of chips, kids could eat carrots, cherry tomatoes, and strawberries that they grew themselves. My babcia in Poland always says that food you grow yourself makes you strong.

Third, a garden would make recess better. Not everyone likes to play soccer or tag. Some kids would love to dig, plant, and look for ladybugs instead.

Some people might think that a garden would attract pests like rabbits. We could build a short fence around the beds and plant marigolds, which some gardeners say keep bugs away.

To conclude, a garden would be a wonderful thing for our school. I hope the principal says yes!
