# Tessa O'Callan: Should our school have a garden? Explain your opinion.

I believe our school should have a garden because it would teach us patience, give us fresh food, and help us feel proud of our school.

To start, a garden teaches patience. You cannot plant a seed on Monday and eat a carrot on Friday. Carrots take about ten weeks to grow. Waiting for something good is hard, but it is an important thing to learn.

Next, a garden gives us fresh food. I have two gardens in my life. My mom grows herbs in pots on our balcony, and my dad has a big vegetable patch. I only see my dad's garden every other weekend because of the custody schedule, so sometimes I miss the best part when the beans are ready. If our school had a garden, I could check on it every single day.

Finally, a garden would make us proud. When visitors come to our school, we could give them a tour and show them what we grew. Our school would be known as the school with the amazing garden.

Some people might say a garden is a lot of work for kids. But we already do hard things, like long division, so I think we can handle some weeding.

In conclusion, a garden would help us grow as much as the plants. I think we should start one as soon as possible.
