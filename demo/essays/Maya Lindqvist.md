# Maya Lindqvist: Should our school have a garden? Explain your opinion.

I think we should have a garden at school. I like flowers and plants a lot.

My favorite flowers are tulips. My mom grows them in front of our house and they come up every spring. They are red and yellow and pink. I would want to plant tulips in the school garden so it is pretty.

Gardens are good because you get fresh air. I have asthma so sometimes I can not run at recess, but I could still do the garden because it is not running. Gardening is calm and you can go slow. That is good for kids who can not run around a lot.

We could also grow food like carrots and peas. Peas are fun to open. I like the sound they make when you pop them.

I think the garden should have a bench so people can sit down and look at it. And maybe a bird feeder.

The garden would make the school happy. Every kid would like it, even kids who dont like vegetables, because there would be flowers too.

That is my opinion. We should have a garden.
